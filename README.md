# In Your Eyes

Category: Forensics  |

## The challenge
I cannot see the colours that you see,
yet my fingers read the shape of every word.
The message is not painted on the surface.
It sits in the smallest part of the light.
What do my fingertips see?

The flag is in the usual format, with the words separated by underscores.

## Flag format
Flags look like `ARGHYA{some_words_here}`. Submit the whole flag, including `ARGHYA{` and `}`,
on the scoring platform. Flags for this challenge are case-insensitive.
